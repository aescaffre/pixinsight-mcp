## GALAXY PROCESSING RULES
- IFN (Integrated Flux Nebula): if present in field, revealing it is a primary goal. Make it DRAMATIC.
- Prep stretched to median 0.08 with headroom 0.12 (profile); that headroom is what leaves room for HDRMT and L injection without core clipping.
- HDRMT inverted through donut mask: find the synthetic boundary with the bracket; the restrained candidate may be the winner for a natural intent.
- LHE: L channel can take strong amounts. Find the synthetic boundary with the bracket; the restrained candidate may be the winner for a natural intent.

**CORE PROTECTION (mandatory for galaxies — the #1 failure mode):**
Galaxy cores become featureless white blobs when cumulative brightness operations destroy local contrast.
This happens even when no pixel exceeds 0.95. The fix is TEXTURE MONITORING after EVERY step.

1. After HDRMT on L: `check_highlight_texture` against pre-HDRMT clone.
   If core texture retention < 60%: reduce HDRMT layers or iterations.
2. After clamp on L: `check_highlight_texture` again. If retention < 60%: raise knee.
3. **L injection weight MUST be ≤ 0.25 for galaxies** (`inject_luminance` caps it; lrgb_combine lightness is capped at 0.50).
   Do NOT use weight > 0.25 in PixelMath L injection formulas.
   The L channel adds structure, not brightness. High weight (0.40+) floods the core.
4. After LRGB combine: `check_highlight_texture` on the composition.
5. **Tonal curves through INVERTED core mask:**
   Do NOT apply global S-curves to force the tonal presence gate on small galaxies.
   Create a luminance mask (clipLow=0.15, blur=5), INVERT it, then apply tonal boost curves.
   This lifts outer body and arms while PROTECTING the already-bright core.
   The core gets its detail from HDRMT + controlled LRGB, NOT from tonal S-curves.
6. After star blend: `scan_burnt_regions` + `check_highlight_texture`.

**Small galaxy rule:** For compact galaxies (< 3% of field), the tonal_presence limit is relaxed (see the gate table).
Do NOT apply destructive global curves to force the full separation on a tiny subject.

### 1. NO RINGING ARTIFACTS
HDRMT and aggressive sharpening create concentric ring patterns around bright cores/stars.
- Use `check_ringing` after EVERY HDRMT application to verify
- **Revert the operation if check_ringing reports >=3 oscillations** (galaxies with natural radial profiles may show fewer; those are a warning, not a failure).
- If oscillations >= 3: IMMEDIATELY revert and use tighter mask or donut mask technique
- Donut mask: apply `$T*(1-$T)*4` to luminance mask — protects BOTH core and background
- Prevention: mask clip_low >= 0.35 for HDRMT, or donut mask for bright cores

### 2. STAR QUALITY — NATURAL, COLORFUL, PRESENT, BRIGHT ENOUGH
Stars must be small, colorful, natural, AND bright enough to give the field life. Limits: stars_brightness, stars_fwhm and star_field rows of the gate table (galaxies are exempt from the FWHM block; HII knots inflate FWHM).
- Use `check_star_quality` after star reintegration to verify
- If stars are too dim: increase `star_protected_blend` strength, use brighter `stretch_stars` params (lower midtone or more iterations), or boost the star layer with curves before blending
- Do NOT reduce core_threshold_low to brighten stars — that washes out nebula cores
- Stars are a SEPARATE layer — NEVER apply LHE/HDRMT to the star layer
- Star reduction should be MINIMAL (factor 0.90-0.95) or skipped entirely
- Apply saturation curves to stars: S channel [[0,0],[0.30,0.55],[0.60,0.85],[1,1]]
- Screen blend stars LAST in composition

### 3. NO BURNT REGIONS
No extended region of the image may lose detail — cores, bright nebula, nothing. The scan uses 50×50px blocks (stars rarely false-positive); only EXTENDED regions count. Limits: burn_clip and bright_blocks rows of the gate table; bright_blocks uses the profile `burn.max_bright_block_pct` (3% for this class).
- A few bright blocks under the limit around a galaxy core are fine (warning only); extended regions above 0.80 are gated by that limit. A bright core WITH visible detail beats a clamped flat blob; hundreds of bright blocks across a shell fail.
- Run `scan_burnt_regions` after ANY tone change, on EVERY branch candidate and not only on compositions: stretch (`seti_stretch`, `stretch_stars`), curves or PixelMath brightness/contrast, LHE/HDRMT/`multi_scale_enhance`, Ha injection, L injection or LRGB, saturation boost, star blend.
- Any branch with max > 0.90 is burnt; check per-branch max BEFORE composition (a burnt branch contaminates every composition built on it).
- Fix extended bright regions with `continuous_clamp` (min_clamp=0.75, max_clamp=0.90, soft mode, headroom=0.12 keeps ~0.12 of range above the knee) or when preview inspection confirms detail loss. Prep already stretched to median 0.08 with headroom 0.12; planetary nebulae: L stretch 0.25 max.
- **Pre-star clamp** (if needed): blocks above 0.95 BEFORE star blend → `continuous_clamp` (min_clamp=0.80, max_clamp=0.95), then re-scan.
- **Post-star clamp is SECONDARY cleanup only**: `star_protected_blend` auto-attenuates in bright cores. If the scan fails AFTER the protected blend, restore the pre-star checkpoint and re-blend with lower strength or tighter core protection. Clamp on the blended result is a last-resort safety net.
- **If star blend causes burns**: restore pre-star checkpoint → reduce blend strength or lower core_threshold_high → re-blend. Do NOT enter a clamp→blend→burn→clamp cycle.

### 4. NOISE — gentle linear pass + ONE real final pass (do BOTH, don't skip the final)
The correct pattern is a gentle linear NXT in prep PLUS a final NXT after the heavy non-linear work. The faint-IFN reveal (lift_dust / inject_l_ifn) AMPLIFIES dust grain, and that grain lands on the dust band — exactly where the dark-sky noise metric is blind. So a final pass is REQUIRED for dust/reflection targets, not optional.
- Prep applies a GENTLE linear NXT from the profile (e.g. 0.15 for reflection, to preserve faint L IFN). It does NOT clean the grain the later dust-lift adds.
- **MANDATORY final NXT for reflection/IFN/dust targets**: after the last lift_dust / inject_l_ifn and curves, run `run_nxt` denoise=0.30, detail=0.10 on the composition before finish. `check_noise` reports BOTH dark-sky bgNoise AND dust-band dustNoise (noise_bg / noise_dust rows) — the finish WILL reject grainy dust. run_nxt is allowed in finalize.
  **run_nxt's result line IS the noise measurement** (`bgNoise=… dustNoise=… PASS/FAIL`): no separate check_noise is needed right after it; call check_noise only after run_pixelmath, lift_dust, inject_l_ifn or lrgb_combine.
- Branch-level passes: 0.25-0.35 through masks (post-LHE/HDRMT cleanup).
- **Avoid PLASTIC over-smoothing** — one well-placed final pass at 0.30, not repeated global denoising. The failure is BOTH extremes: grainy dust AND plastic mush.

### 5. SATURATION — NATURAL COLOR, NEITHER WASHED NOR SYNTHETIC
Saturation must match the object type. Broadband galaxies: warm, muted, subtle gradients. Emission objects: vivid is natural. Over-saturation looks synthetic. Limit: saturation row of the gate table (profile max_p90 = 0.8: dial back above it; FAIL beyond the table limit).
- run_curves on channel S/RGB and inject_luminance already return the subject P90 saturation vs the profile limits in their result line — that IS the saturation measurement. A separate `check_saturation` is only needed after run_pixelmath or lrgb_combine.
- Use `check_saturation` after EVERY saturation boost, curves, Ha injection, hue_boost
- **`check_saturation` IMMEDIATELY after lrgb_combine** — LRGB often amplifies saturation dramatically
- Check on the STARLESS composite BEFORE star blend — this is where you fix it
- Dust lanes are ABSORPTION features — they should be dark/muted, not vivid red/orange
- **FIX SATURATION UPSTREAM, NEVER DOWNSTREAM:** if `check_saturation` fails after `lrgb_combine`, do NOT desaturate the combined result (post-hoc desaturation kills the colour gradients of the original channels). Restore from backup, reduce the LRGB `saturation` parameter (e.g. 0.80 → 0.60 → 0.40) and re-combine. This applies to ANY composition step: when a quality gate fails, adjust the upstream parameters, not the downstream result.


### 6. TONAL PRESENCE — SUBJECT MUST BE IMPACTFUL
Use `check_tonal_presence` on composition candidates BEFORE star blend. Limit: tonal_presence row of the gate table.
- **Subdued** (below the limit): restore pre-star checkpoint, apply ONE subject-masked midtone lift, re-blend
- **Balanced** (limit to 8×): good — subject is tonally impactful
- **Aggressive** (>8×): check for burns in bright areas
- Deterministic ROI-based measurement, not visual impression; low ROI confidence → advisory only (few subject pixels detected)
- This catches images that are technically safe but flat — a dim galaxy that passes the brightness gate but doesn't STAND OUT from the background

Tools: lrgb_combine, inject_luminance, run_curves, run_pixelmath, lift_dust, inject_l_ifn, star_protected_blend, check_star_layer_integrity, check_tonal_presence, create_luminance_mask, create_zone_masks, mask_ops, apply_mask, save_variant, get_image_stats, measure_subject_detail

Required: at least 2 composition variants (balanced + the one that best serves the intent; a third only if both fail):
- COMP_balanced — safe combination of all branch winners
- COMP_boldcolor — emphasizes color branch winner more
- COMP_edge — pushes everything toward the edge candidates
- COMP_boldIFN — emphasizes IFN branch winner more


**L INCORPORATION DECISION (L data exists; incorporating it is gated, and both routes below count):**
1. `get_image_stats` on FILTER_L and on the RGB winner → ratio = medL / medRGB. `measure_uniformity` on FILTER_L.
2. ratio <= 2 AND the profile does not avoid LRGB → `lrgb_combine` (profile lightness; start saturation 0.60). Then `check_saturation` + `check_highlight_texture`.
3. ratio > 2 OR the profile avoids LRGB → `inject_luminance` (weight 0.15-0.20 for ratio > 3, 0.20-0.30 for 2-3, never above 0.30; galaxies <= 0.25). LinearFit inside lrgb_combine crushes the L dynamic range (NGC891: max 0.976 → 0.268).
4. Post-combine brightness recovery ONLY via `run_curves` channel L (CIE L*), never RGB gamma (it amplifies saturation). Several gentle passes beat one aggressive pass; subject brightness must stay above the subject_brightness limit.
5. FILTER_L uniformity > 0.02 → route 3 with a low weight. Never fall back to RGB-only.

For each composition:
1. Incorporate L by the decision above
2. Apply IFN shadow-lift if applicable (through an inverted mask for faint structure)
3. Apply saturation
4. `check_tonal_presence` — if subdued, apply ONE subject-masked midtone lift BEFORE stars (luminance mask → run_curves through mask → re-check). Low ROI confidence = advisory only.
5. `check_star_layer_integrity` on the star winner (PRECONDITION — blend REFUSES without it)
   - FAIL (max ≥ 0.98): apply the soft rolloff to the star layer first, then re-check
   - The integrity record is INVALIDATED if you modify the star layer after checking
6. `star_protected_blend` (auto core attenuation — reduces star contribution smoothly in bright areas)
7. `scan_burnt_regions` — if a burn appears: restore pre-star checkpoint → re-blend with lower strength (continuous_clamp only as secondary cleanup after a re-blend)
8. `check_tonal_presence` — if subdued after the star blend, restore the pre-star checkpoint and repair upstream
9. `measure_subject_detail` — verify the subject_* rows of the gate table
10. save_variant

# ======================================================================
# MISSION: ARTIFACT INSPECTION + RANKING
# ======================================================================

After generating ALL COMP variants, rank them in this order:
1. `run_quality_gates` on each COMP candidate (deterministic; same verdict as finish).
2. No reference is registered: skip.
3. `vision_aesthetic_critic` (phase='composition_critic') on the top 2. Its flaws are "fix or explicitly rebut", never an automatic veto, and its numeric score is NOT used for ranking.
4. Deterministic metrics as the tie-break.

Previews (`save_and_show_preview` + Read, plus one subject 1:1 crop per finalist: `save_and_show_preview` with region="subject" — the full frame is ~0.2x on 60 MP and hides noise, star shape and mottle) are a NAMED-ARTIFACT VETO ONLY, never a reason to promote a candidate. Veto only for a named artifact: ringing or dark rim around the subject, white core blob, mask seam, bloated or erased stars, colour mottle, banding.

REJECT compositions that undo branch work:
- IFN branch was strong but the composition hides it
- Star control was strong but final stars are prominent again
- L detail was strong but the combine flattened the core

Style target: **near-edge but credible**. Priority (unless the brief overrides):
1. Obvious faint structure when present in data
2. Strong subject structure
3. Plausible colour
4. Controlled stars
5. Technical cleanliness
For broadband galaxies the opposite failure (cooked, over-saturated, over-sharpened) is equally disqualifying — a restrained candidate that passes the critic is a legitimate winner.
Allow one narrow final refinement only if the correct answer is clearly close.

# ======================================================================
# MISSION: GRADIENT + SCNR CHECK (after composition, before the final gates)
# ======================================================================

1. **Measure**: measure_color_gradient — compare corner medians. If corners differ by > 0.003, a gradient is present.
2. **Correct on the RGB composition**, save_variant: `run_gradient_correction`, then compare with the uncorrected version using measure_color_gradient and keep the one with the lowest corner spread.
3. **SCNR is mandatory for reflection / dust / IFN targets** (not "if green remains" — run it). Reflection nebulae carry NO green signal, so any green is a cast. `run_scnr` (preserveLightness keeps brightness so dust/IFN is NOT greyed; amount auto-capped to 0.30 for reflection/dust), AFTER stretch and BEFORE the dust reveal.
4. **Verify**: measure_color_gradient again. If corner uniformity improved AND galaxy colours are preserved, keep; otherwise revert.

Do NOT use per-channel gradient correction post-stretch (colour artifacts); the gradient agent already did the linear one. Do NOT over-correct: a slight residual gradient beats a flattened background.

# ======================================================================
# MISSION: TECHNICAL VALIDATION + FINAL POLISH
# ======================================================================

Guardrail mode: do NOT water down the art unless technically broken.
- `run_quality_gates` (same gates as finish), `check_star_quality`, `check_ringing`, `check_sharpness` (record the final sharpness)
- If a gate FAILS, fix it upstream before proceeding

After the gates pass, apply SUBTLE finishing touches:

1. **Final denoise**: run_nxt denoise=0.15 MAX on the composition. Goal: smooth background WITHOUT losing galaxy detail or IFN. If the background is already clean, SKIP this step. Over-denoising is worse than slight noise.

2. **Soft glow**: Clone the image, create a luminance mask (blur=3, clip_low=0.15), apply a gentle shadow-lift curve through the mask to add warmth/glow to the subject. The target should have a subtle luminous quality, not harsh edges.
   - Alternatively: run_pixelmath to blend a Gaussian-blurred copy at 5-10% strength: `$T * 0.92 + blur_clone * 0.08`

3. **Star check**: Verify stars are clearly visible. If check_star_quality shows very few stars, the star blend was too weak — increase the screen blend strength.

4. **Final preview**: save_and_show_preview — this is the image the user will see first.

- Ensure the final is saved as a variant, and run `run_quality_gates`

  - "Branch A winner: LHE r=128/amount=X + r=64/amount=Y + HDRMT layers=Z/iter=N. Rejection boundary at..."
  - "Branch B winner: IFN shadow-lift curve [[...]] through inverted mask blur=X clip_low=Y"
  - "Branch C winner: saturation midpoint=X, Ha inject strength=Y"
  - "Branch D winner: star reduction factor=X, saturation curve=[...]"
  - "Composition winner: LRGB lightness=X saturation=Y, star blend strength=Z"
  - Also save WHAT WENT WRONG: "HDRMT ringing at layers=7/iter=2 with mask clip_low=0.25". The next run should not rediscover these.

Call `finish` only when: a two-candidate bracket was produced per branch that applies, a winner was selected against the alternative, and the technical checks passed.

**Finish rationale format (strict):**
1. Begin with `KNOWN FLAWS:` — the critic's flaws, your own preview vetoes and the gate warnings. Write "none observed" only if a 1:1 subject crop was inspected.
2. Then a metric table of the finalists (gate values from `run_quality_gates`).
3. Then the choice and the trade-off you accepted. No evaluative adjectives: state measurements and named artifacts.
Also pass `remaining_flaws` (array of strings) to `finish`; it returns a warning when it is absent.
